# Buying a car — Bellevue, WA — Sep 2026

## The brief

Primary reason: convenience. Public transport limits where we go. We want to
stop planning trips around it.

Secondary: this is a large purchase. We do not want to skimp.

## Criteria, in the owner's stated order

1. **Reliable.** No large service bill every few months.
2. **Value for money.**
3. **Holds its value.**
4. **Spacious.** A future kid and passengers must fit easily. No sedans.
5. **Low APR** is a bonus, not a requirement.
6. **Prefer a hybrid** over pure petrol. Added 11 Sep 2026.

On criterion 6: a **48-volt mild hybrid does not count**. BMW and Mercedes label
the X3 and the GLC as hybrids. Those systems cannot drive the car on
electricity and return very little fuel saving. Only a full hybrid or a
plug-in hybrid satisfies this criterion.

## Constraints

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Location | Bellevue, WA (King County, Sound Transit RTA district) |
| Budget | $40k–$60k. $65k on-road is a soft ceiling |
| Home charging | **None.** Assigned parking spot, no outlet |
| Other charging | Office (Amazon). Unconfirmed. Employer-dependent |
| Driver | Experienced. Comfortable driving for hours |
| Hold period | Assume long. Treat 7–10 years as the planning horizon |

## Where this stands

Test drives booked for 12 Sep 2026: Tesla, and Mazda CX-5/CX-50.

Research changed the shortlist. See [research/market-sep-2026.md](research/market-sep-2026.md).
The short version:

- The **2026 CX-5 is a first-year redesign with documented defects.** It fails
  criterion 1. It also offers **no hybrid until 2027**, so it now fails
  criterion 6 as well. Drive the CX-50 instead.
- **Mazda fell in the latest Consumer Reports reliability report. Tesla rose.**
  This reverses the assumption behind the original plan.
- The budget **overshoots** both original candidates by roughly $15k–$20k.
- Washington removed its EV sales-tax exemption in Sep 2025. The federal credit
  is also gone. An EV no longer carries a purchase-price subsidy here.

equivalent SUV. It normally costs less. The decision stands on size and feel,
which are valid grounds. Record the correct cost fact in case the question
returns.
