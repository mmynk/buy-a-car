# 2026 RAV4 — trims, moonroof, colour — 12 Sep 2026

Read from Toyota's own per-grade data on `toyota.com/rav4/features/` and
`toyota.com/rav4pluginhybrid/features/`, then confirmed line-by-line against
**48 dealer window stickers**.

## The short answers

1. **Yes, your XLE Premium FWD can have a moonroof.** Panoramic, package code
   **SR, $1,850**. You do not have to change trim.
2. **Yes, both colours you would want are on the XLE Premium.** Storm Cloud costs
   **$0**. Wind Chill Pearl costs **$475**. The XLE Premium has the widest colour
   choice of any grade.
3. **Colour barely affects resale.** Act on preference.

## The moonroof, in detail

Verbatim from a Monroney sticker for VIN `2T36DRBV0TW029168`, an XLE Premium
hybrid FWD, dated 10 Sep 2026:

```
SR   Panoramic Moonroof Package:                 1,850.00
     - Power Tilt/Slide Panoramic Moonroof
     - Digital Rearview Mirror w/ Homelink
```

The package contains exactly those two items. The same `SR / $1,850` line
appeared on five XLE Premium stickers at two dealers, and KBB agrees.

**Panoramic roof availability:** XLE Premium (option), XSE (option), Limited
(standard), plug-in XSE (option). Not on LE, SE, Woodland or GR Sport.

**Cheapest 2026 RAV4 with any moonroof: SE FWD at $34,700 + $850 = $35,550** —
which is **$550 less than the XLE Premium base alone**. What you give up: SofTex
trim, Digital Key, wireless charging, and the standard power liftgate (the SE
needs a $400 Convenience Package for that). You keep heated front seats and the
8-way power driver's seat. The SE's roof is plain, not panoramic.

## Does a moonroof cost rear headroom? No source says.

**An earlier note in this workspace warned that a moonroof usually costs rear
headroom. That was overstated. Correcting it here.**

The honest position:

- Toyota publishes **one** headroom row for the RAV4: "Head room with moonroof
  (max) = 36.7 in", identical across all six trims, with no front/rear split.
  **That figure is almost certainly corrupt** — it equals the cargo-height figure
  on the same page, while every sibling row is a front/rear pair. Do not use it.
- KBB gives front 37.9 in and no rear row. Car and Driver gives second row
  39.5 in and no front row. **Neither splits by roof.**
- The previous-generation RAV4 published `37.7 / 39.5` front/rear **labelled "with
  moonroof."** Toyota has never published a without-moonroof RAV4 figure, so the
  number already in circulation was the pessimistic one. **There is no larger
  no-roof figure to lose.**
- Toyota **does** split it on other models, and the effect is model-specific:
  on the 2026 Camry a panoramic roof costs **0.8 in at the front and 0.0 in at
  the rear**; on the Highlander Platinum it costs 1.2 in front and 2.3 in the
  second row. Neither transfers to the RAV4.

Two structural points in your favour: the sliding panel sits over the **front**
row, and the second-row glass is **fixed**. And for a rear-facing seat the
binding constraint is front-to-rear clearance, not rear headroom.

**One open conflict.** BabyDrive, 9 Jul 2026, fitted three rear-facing seats and
reported that a 186 cm driver still fits in front — better than the old RAV4.
But Cars.com published **"The 2026 RAV4 Performed Worse in Our Car Seat Check
Than the 2025"** on 2 Mar 2026. cars.com is blocked here, so **only the headline
was read.** Resolve this yourself with the physical test.

## Does colour affect resale? Real, and a rounding error

**Best available study is 2025, not 2026.** iSeeCars, *The Best and Worst Car
Colors for Resale Value*, `iseecars.com/car-color-study`. Sample **1.2 million
model-year-2022 used cars** listed Aug 2024 – May 2025.

iSeeCars did publish a **June 2026** colour study, but it covers **popularity
only** and contains no depreciation figures. **No 2026 depreciation-by-colour
data exists.**

Three-year depreciation, all body styles: Yellow 24.0% best, Orange 24.4%,
Green 26.3%, Beige 29.5%, Red 29.8%, Silver 29.8%, Brown 30.4%, Gray 30.5%,
**Blue 30.9%**, average 31.0%, Black 31.9%, White 32.1%, Gold 34.4% worst.

**The SUV table is the one that applies, and it erases the advantage.** SUV
average 32.7%; **blue is 9th of 12 at 33.1%, which is 0.4 points worse than the
SUV average.**

**Verdict: buy the colour you like.** 0.4 points is noise beside trim, mileage
and condition. For scale, the RAV4's model-level depreciation is 25.2% against
37.9–39.3% for the Mazdas — a 13-point gap. **Choosing the RAV4 is worth roughly
30 times more than choosing its colour.** The study also controls for neither
trim nor model, so its winning colours are really Corvettes and Raptors.
