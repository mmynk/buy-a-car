# 2026 prices — verified 12 Sep 2026

## Read the basis first

Prices differ by $1,300–$1,600 depending on the source, because of destination
charges.

| Source | Basis |
|---|---|
| **KBB trim tables** | MSRP **including** destination |
| **Manufacturer sites** | MSRP **excluding** destination |
| **Car and Driver** | Includes destination, runs ~$500 above KBB on Toyota and Lexus |

Destination fees: **Mazda $1,530** (confirmed by arithmetic on all 9 CX-90
trims). Toyota **$1,450 or $1,495** — sources conflict. Lexus **$1,295**, from a
stale page.

**Prices below are KBB, so they include destination.** On-road adds roughly
**12.9%** in Bellevue on top.

## Eliminations this data forces

### The entire Mazda CX-90 line is out

**The inline-6 is a mild hybrid.** KBB: "augmented by a **mild-hybrid system**."
Mazda's own site: "**incorporating a mild hybrid powertrain**." It cannot drive
the car on electricity in any meaningful way. It fails criterion 6.

**The PHEV is the 4th least reliable car in America** per Consumer Reports. It
fails criterion 1.

Both versions fail a different criterion. There is no configuration to buy.

This was the only mild hybrid found in the whole set. No other candidate uses
one.

### The Highlander Hybrid is strictly dominated

It is **worse and more expensive** than the Grand Highlander Hybrid.

| Measure | Highlander Hybrid | Grand Highlander Hybrid |
|---|---|---|
| Base price | **$49,115** | **$46,205** |
| Third-row legroom | **28.0 in** | **33.5 in** |
| Cargo behind row 3 | 16.0 cu ft | 20.6 cu ft |
| Max cargo | 84.3 cu ft | 97.5 cu ft |

KBB on the Highlander's third row: "**best left to smaller children**"; "even if
they're all kids, fitting three people back there is a tight squeeze."

**The Grand Highlander is the real 3-row. The Highlander is not.** Pay less, get
5.5 more inches. There is no argument for the Highlander.

### Lexus TX and the plug-in Lexus models miss the budget

- **TX 500h $71,410 and TX 550h+ $81,760** — both far over the ceiling. Worse,
  **both hybrids seat 6 only.** Seven seats require the pure-petrol TX 350,
  which fails criterion 6.
- **RX 450h+ PHEV $66,680** — over the ceiling before tax.
- **NX 450h+ PHEV $59,105** — about **$66,700 on the road**, so just over.

Flag: four outlets ran "Lexus drops the TX 550h+" headlines in June 2026. No
article body was readable. KBB and Car and Driver still list it. Unresolved.

### Kia Telluride is not buyable in the window

**No 2026 Telluride exists.** Both KBB and Car and Driver skip from 2025 to
2027, and the Telluride has no row in NHTSA's MY2026 report. **The hybrid is
2027-only.** It shares the Palisade's 329 hp powertrain.

## The models that survive, in detail

### Toyota Grand Highlander Hybrid — the 3-row answer

| Trim | Powertrain | MSRP |
|---|---|---|
| **LE** | 245 hp HEV | **$46,205** |
| **XLE** | 245 hp HEV | **$47,375** |
| Limited | 245 hp HEV | $53,705 |
| Nightshade | 245 hp HEV | $54,685 |
| Hybrid MAX Limited | **362 hp** HEV | $56,685 |
| Hybrid MAX Platinum | 362 hp HEV | $60,770 |

- **AWD is standard on every trim except the XLE**, where it is a $1,600 option.
  Since AWD is not needed, **the XLE in front-wheel drive is the efficiency pick
  at 36 mpg** against 34 for AWD.
- Hybrid MAX costs 7–9 mpg for its extra power. 27 mpg combined.
- Assembled in **Princeton, Indiana**.
- Not a first-year design.

204 hp, 2.0-litre with two motors, 37–40 mpg combined. **No CR-V plug-in

**The cheapest full hybrid on the list, and the cheapest to run at $407/year.**

### Toyota RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid

| Trim | Ex-destination | EV range | MPG / MPGe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SE** | $41,500 | **54 mi** | 40 / 101 |
| Woodland | $45,300 | 49 mi | 37 / 94 |
| XSE | $47,200 | 52 mi | 40 / 96 |
| GR Sport | $48,500 | 49 mi | 36 / 86 |

- **324 hp on every trim.** No variance.
- Called "RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid" now. "Prime" is gone. No Limited PHEV.
- **AWD only.** No front-drive version.
- **KBB's sidebar figure of 42 miles is stale 2025 data.** The body says 54.
- Assembled in **Takaoka, Japan**.
- Pays the **$225/year** Washington surcharge, not $75.

### Hyundai Palisade Hybrid — the value 3-row

**All-new for 2026, second generation, and the first Palisade hybrid ever.**
Range **$45,760–$60,380**. 329 hp, 2.5-litre turbo, 6-speed automatic.

- **Front-wheel drive is worth up to 5 mpg here** — 34 combined on the "Blue"
  trims, 31 on upper front-drive trims, **29 on every AWD model.** The largest
  AWD penalty in this set.
- Third-row legroom 32.1 in, so close to the Grand Highlander's 33.5 in.
- Assembled in **Korea**.
- Caveats: first-year generation, Hyundai is **12th** in Consumer Reports
  reliability, and it won **no** KBB resale award.
- Per-trim MSRP **could not be verified** — KBB shows only Fair Purchase Prices.

### Subaru Forester Hybrid

Hybrid trims: Premium $36,180 · Sport $39,380 · Limited $40,445 · Touring
$42,995. 194 hp, **35 mpg**, series-parallel full hybrid. Subaru **cut prices in
February 2026**.

**AWD is standard, with no front-drive version**, so its main advantage is one
this buyer does not need. RepairPal puts it at **$632/year**, the worst of the
mainstream options, and it won no KBB resale award.
